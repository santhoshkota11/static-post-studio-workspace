CAPTION
-------
Before you pay the token amount, spend 20 minutes on the MahaRERA website. 🏠

Most first-time buyers in Pune check the brochure, the sample flat and the loan offer. Very few check what the developer has actually filed with RERA. That's where the real story is.

5 things to look up:
1. Registration number & validity: does it match this exact project and phase?
2. Approved plans: what is the developer allowed to build, not what the brochure shows?
3. Litigation: any court case on the project or the land?
4. Construction progress: is the building keeping pace with the promised date?
5. Developer track record: how did their older projects go?
