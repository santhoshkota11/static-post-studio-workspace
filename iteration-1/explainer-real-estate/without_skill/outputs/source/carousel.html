Registration number & validity

Every project in Maharashtra must be registered before it is advertised. Search the number from the brochure and make sure it matches this exact project and phase.

The approved plans, not the brochure

Check three

Any litigation on the project

Developers have to disclose court cases on the project or the land. A pending title dispute can hold up possession for years, even if construction looks fine.

Litigation details
Cases related to this projectNo
Cases on the land / titleNo
Encumbrance declaredCheck
Title report uploadedYes

Red flagAny "Yes" here. Don't pay anything until a property lawyer has read the case details.
