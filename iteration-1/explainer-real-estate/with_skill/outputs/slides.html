Check 1 of 5 · Registration

Is the project
registered, and
still valid?

Search it on MahaRERA. Note the registration number and the completion date the builder declared.

Red flag

No registration number on the ad or brochure, or a registration that has lapsed.

02
Check 2 of 5 · Approved plans

Does your flat exist
on the approved plan?

Open the sanctioned plan and commencement certificate. Match your wing, floor and carpet area.

Red flag

Your floor isn’t in the sanctioned plan yet, and you’re told “approval is coming”.

Check 3 of 5 · Land title

Who owns the land,
and is it mortgaged?

Read the legal title report and the encumbrances the builder has declared: loans, liens, disputes.

Red flag

A court case or a loan on the land that the sales team never mentioned.

Check 4 of 5 · Progress

Is construction
keeping pace with
the promise?

Read the latest quarterly progress update: work completed, and flats booked versus total.

Red flag

Updates that stopped coming, or progress stuck at the same stage for quarters.

Check 5 of 5 · Track record

What do other
buyers say on record?

Look up complaints and orders
against this project, and the
builder’s other projects.

Red flag

Repeated complaints about delays, or older projects that are still unfinished.
