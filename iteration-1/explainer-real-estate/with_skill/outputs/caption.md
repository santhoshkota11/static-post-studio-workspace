## Caption
The brochure is what the builder wants you to see. MahaRERA shows what they declared on record.

Before you pay a token amount for your first flat in Pune, spend ten minutes on the MahaRERA website and check five things:

1. Is the project registered, and is the registration still valid?
2. Is your flat on the approved plan?
3. Who owns the land, and is anything mortgaged or in dispute?
4. Is construction keeping pace with the promise?
5. What complaints are on record against the project or the builder?

Save this for your next site visit, and send it to whoever is house-hunting with you.

2. Sand ledger-paper slide, check 1 of 5, registration: "Is the project registered, and still valid?" Search it on MahaRERA and note the registration number and declared completion date. Red flag: no registration number on the ad, or a lapsed registration.
3. Check 2 of 5, approved plans: "Does your flat exist on the approved plan?" Open the sanctioned plan and commencement certificate and match your wing, floor and carpet area. Red flag: your floor isn't sanctioned yet and you're told approval is coming.
4. Check 3 of 5, land title: "Who owns the land, and is it mortgaged?" Read the legal title report and declared encumbrances. Red flag: a court case or loan on the land nobody mentioned.
5. Check 4 of 5, progress: "Is construction keeping pace with the promise?" Read the latest quarterly progress update, work completed and flats booked versus total. Red flag: updates stopped, or progress stuck for quarters.
6. Check 5 of 5, track record: "What do other buyers say on record?" Look up complaints and orders against the project and the builder's other projects. Red flag: repeated delay complaints or unfinished older projects.
