## Identity
- What they do / for whom: specialty coffee bar, Jubilee Hills, Hyderabad; for the craft-coffee crowd
- Voice: calm, precise, a little wry; never shouty ("NEW!!!", emoji, exclamation marks)
- Handle / website / CTA preference: handle TBC; CTAs = tag / save / visit the bar
- Category codes kept: flat colour worlds, display-serif drink names, menu/label grammar. Broken: no stock photos, no hype stickers

## Assets
- Logo: not supplied. Use the name set in Instrument Serif, 40 px, top-left
- Fonts: Instrument Serif (display), Inter Tight (text), IBM Plex Mono (labels), all Google Fonts
- Photos: none yet (product shoot week of 23 Nov)

## System
- Canvas 1080×1350 (4:5); margin 88 px; spacing unit 8 px; kicker line at y 232
- Palette: roast black `#14110F` (field) · cream `#F2EBE1` (ink on dark) · `#A39A8E` (muted on dark) ·
  paper `#EFE7DB` (light field) · `#6B6258` (muted on paper) · burnt orange `#D2561E` (accent ~10%; on-accent ink = roast black)
- Type: Instrument Serif 132 / 184 (names) / 56 (italic tags); Inter Tight 34–36 body; Plex Mono 24 caps +14% labels
- Emphasis: italic switch only
- Layouts: type statement, number hero, label/spec card
- Motif: the 14-hour steep dial (14 ticks + minor ticks, orange arc ending in a dot); big and cut by the edge, or small as a seal
- Finish: fine grain; faint warm light pool on black
- Furniture: wordmark top-left, mono counter top-right, "→" swipe cue in mono, one CTA

## Do / Don't
- Do: one idea per slide, lots of black, sentence case, real prices and dates
- Don't: drop shadows, gradients as decoration, emoji, badges, AI product images
