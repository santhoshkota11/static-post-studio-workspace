## 1. Strategy
```
Objective:      footfall at the bar from launch Friday, plus saves and tags that spread the date
Audience:       Jubilee Hills / Banjara Hills specialty-coffee crowd, 22–40; already know cold brew, have seen a
                hundred "NEW!" cold-brew posters, and care about craft, provenance and taste
Single message: Second Crack's new cold brews are made with time, not heat, and you can only get them at the bar
Insight:        The bar is named after "second crack", the hottest, loudest moment in a roast. A cold brew is the
                opposite of that. The tension between the name and the product is the brand's own story, and no
                other café can tell it
Big idea:       "Named after the heat. Brewed without it."  The 14-hour steep, drawn as a 14-tick dial, is the one motif
Format:         carousel, 6 slides, 4:5 (most feed space, boostable). Type-led because there are no photos yet
Story arc:      Showcase / menu (concept.md arc 10) bookended by a hook and a CTA:
                1 hook (the name vs the product) → 2 the method (14 hours, no heat: second cover) →
                3–5 one flavour per slide, as a bottle-label spec card → 6 price, place, date, one action
```

## 2. Concept routes
1. **Chosen: "Named after the heat. Brewed without it."** (type-led + motif) Cover line as written. The cafe's name
   becomes the hook, the 14-hour dial carries the idea visually, and the flavour slides read like premium bottle labels.
   Why: only Second Crack can say it. It works with no photography and still looks crafted, not like a template.
2. **Alternate: "The 14-hour menu"** (format-led: a brew log). Each slide is a page of a handwritten-feel brew log
   (time stamps 8 pm → 10 am, "steep check", tasting notes), ending on "ready Friday". Cover: "We started this one
   14 hours ago." Charming, but it needs real process details we don't have, and it can tip into craft-zine cute.
3. **Alternate: "Three ways to not drink it hot"** (image-led, for after the shoot). Hero bottle/glass on a flat
   burnt-orange world, alternating with macro slides (jaggery crystals, cardamom pods, kokum rind in tonic). Cover:
   "Three ways to skip the heat." This needs the shoot, so it is the natural **launch-day follow-up post** (see §7).

## 3. System spec
- **Canvas** 1080×1350, margin 88 px, spacing unit 8 px. Furniture is fixed on every slide: wordmark top-left
  (88, 88), counter top-right (mono), kicker line at y = 232.
- **Palette** (roles · hex · contrast)
  - field / roast black `#14110F` (60%) · warm, not pure black. A very faint orange light pool (7%) keeps it reading as a surface
  - ink on dark `#F2EBE1` (15.9:1) · muted on dark `#A39A8E` (6.8:1)
  - paper field `#EFE7DB` (slide 2 only) · ink `#14110F` (15.3:1) · muted `#6B6258` (4.9:1)
  - accent / burnt orange `#D2561E` (~10%) · on black 4.56:1 (passes body), on paper 3.36:1 (display only; used only for the 420 px "14")
  - orange field (close slide) with roast-black ink, 4.56:1
- **Type** Instrument Serif (display; names, headlines, price, wordmark) + Inter Tight (body / spec values) +
  IBM Plex Mono as the utility label face (kickers, spec labels, counter). Sizes: h1 132 · flavour name 184 ·
  hero numeral 420 · tag 56 · body 34–36 · labels 24 (caps, +14% tracking). Display tracking −1.5% to −2.5%, leading 0.9–0.96.
- **Emphasis device (one):** the italic switch: *Brewed without it*, *Just time*, *Cardamom*, *Tonic*, *Only at the bar*.
- **Layouts:** type statement (1, 6), number-as-hero (2), label / spec card ×3 (3–5).
  Rhythm: black → paper → black ×3 → orange. Slide 6 bookends slide 1 (same dial position).
- **Motif:** the **14-hour steep dial**: 14 hour ticks plus quarter-hour minor ticks, and an orange arc from 0 to 14 h
  ending in a dot (brew done). Large and cut by the edge on 1, 2 and 6. **Seam:** the cover's dial is centred
  on the slide edge, so its other half opens slide 2 and pulls the swipe. Small, as a label seal with the flavour number, on 3–5.
- **Finish:** fine grain on every slide (screen on black, multiply on paper/orange). No shadows, gradients, stickers or emoji.
- **Imagery:** none in this post (type-led on purpose). Real photos come in the follow-up post after the shoot.
- **Furniture:** wordmark in type, never redrawn as a logo; mono counter 01/06; swipe cue "Meet the three →"
  plus the dial cut by the edge; CTA = one action (tag).

## 7. QA and critique (after three render loops)
- **Fixed:** the dial collided with "No heat." on slide 2 (both dials moved down 100 px); the three-name list overlapped
  the headline on slide 6; a broken "14 / hours" line and an orphan "bar." on the cover; the CTA wrapped into a
  narrow column. Flavour slides went from 7 sizes to 5.
- **Kept on purpose:** QA flags 5 text sizes on slides 3–5 (184 name / 88 seal number + price / 56 tag / 36 spec / 24 labels).
  The 24 px mono labels are the label-format's utility tier, and the seal number and price share one size, so the read
  order stays name → tag → spec → price. The orange shows up 2–3 times on a flavour slide (kicker, dial arc, price),
  all small, about 4% of the area.
- One-second test at 390 px: the cover headline reads, and the dial cut by the edge says "swipe". Grid crop (3:4): nothing essential is lost.
- Truth: no invented tasting notes, origins, awards, address, hours or handle. The taglines are mood lines, not claims.
